## Founding Members

The early days of NSG were made possible by the initiative and dedication of its founding members. Their efforts helped shape the identity, culture, and direction of the community.

**Key figures in the founding of NSG include:**

* **K4LM3D** – Proposed the idea of forming an all-Filipino gang so players from the Philippines could unite and support each other in-game.
* **Skae_19** – Founder of Nousagi Gang (NSG) in *CyberCode Online* on 23 February 2022 and the original leader who built the early membership of the group.

These individuals helped turn a simple idea into a real community that continues to grow today.

## Hall of Legends

Throughout its history, several members have played important roles in helping NSG expand into new games and remain active as a community.

**Notable contributors include:**

* **En_Roise** – Established NSG in *Arclight City* on 19 October 2023, expanding the gang’s presence into another text-based MMORPG.
* **Chief_Marauder** – Proposed the idea of expanding NSG into *Torn (Torn City)*.
* **Xythran** – Working on preparations and planning for the possible establishment of NSG in Torn.
* **K4LM3D** – Continuing efforts to help bring NSG into new gaming worlds.

These members represent the ongoing spirit of NSG: taking initiative, supporting the community, and helping the gang grow across different games.
